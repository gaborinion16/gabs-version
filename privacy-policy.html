Privacy Policy

Gab's Version for Daymap · Last updated 18 November 2026

Gab's Version for Daymap is an unofficial browser extension made by a student. It is not made by, endorsed by or connected to Daymap, Civica or any school.

What the extension does

It changes how Adelaide High School's Daymap website looks in your own browser, and adds extra features: notes, wallpapers, an Edit Mode for changing how it looks, games, a chat feature and an AI helper. It only runs on adelaidehs.daymap.net. It reads the Daymap pages you open so it can redesign them. It never changes anything stored in Daymap.

Kept only on your device

These stay in your browser's storage and are never sent anywhere by the extension:

  • Your notes, sticky notes and checklist
  • Wallpaper, colours, fonts, your Today page layout and header, your sidebar and top buttons, your own links, text boxes, saved looks, your profile picture, bio and note, and any photo or short video you choose as a wallpaper
  • A copy of your teachers' names from your timetable, so they can show on the Today page
  • Daily Word progress, Focus Mode, your best game scores and times, and other settings

Your grades, messages, tasks and other Daymap information are only read to show them on screen. They are never sent anywhere by the extension.

Sent off your device

Some features only work by sending information to an outside service. Each is only used if you choose to use that feature.

Like any website, these services can see your internet address when your browser connects to them.

What the developer never does

  • Sell or share your information with anyone
  • Show ads or track what you do
  • Collect analytics
  • Use your information for anything except making these features work

Other people can see

In online games, Chat and on the game leaderboards, other people see your first name and last initial. People who search for you in Chat can see your full first and last name, so friends can find the right person. Your email address is never shown to other students. Anyone in a chat can screenshot what you write, so don't share private details.

Keeping chat safe

  • Only people with an @schools.sa.edu.au email can use Chat, and you can only chat with people who accepted your friend request.
  • Friend suggestions only show people who share a friend or a group chat with you.
  • Swear words are hidden and slurs are blocked automatically.
  • Anyone can report a message. A report is an email to the developer from your own email account, and it includes the recent messages in that chat so the developer can see what happened.
  • The developer can block an account from Chat. To do that, the developer can see the names and school email addresses of Chat accounts, but cannot read chats they are not in unless a report shows the messages.

Students and parents

This extension is made for high school students. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's leaderboard entry removed, or have a question, please email the developer.

Removing your information

  • Information on your device is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear your browser's site data for Daymap.
  • To remove your entry from a game leaderboard, email the developer.
  • Online games are not saved, so there is nothing to remove.
  • Chat messages delete themselves after 7 days unless a chat has turned this off. Turn it back on in the chat's People panel and older messages are deleted within the hour. To remove your Chat account and friends list, email the developer.
