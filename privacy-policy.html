Privacy Policy

Gab's Version for Daymap · Last updated 3 November 2026

Gab's Version for Daymap is an unofficial browser extension made by a student. It is not made by, endorsed by or connected to Daymap, Civica or any school.

What the extension does

It changes how Adelaide High School's Daymap website looks in your own browser, and adds extra features: notes, wallpapers, games, a chat feature and an AI helper. It only runs on adelaidehs.daymap.net. It reads the Daymap pages you open so it can redesign them. It never changes anything stored in Daymap.

Kept only on your device

These stay in your browser's storage and are never sent anywhere by the extension:

  • Your notes and sticky notes
  • Wallpaper, colours and any photo you choose as a wallpaper
  • A copy of your teachers' names from your timetable, so they can show on the Today page
  • Daily Word progress, your best Snake score and other settings

Your grades, messages, tasks and other Daymap information are only read to show them on screen. They are never sent anywhere by the extension.

Sent off your device

Some features only work by sending information to an outside service. Each is only used if you choose to use that feature.

FeatureWhat is sentWhere it goes
Snake leaderboardYour first name and last initial, your best score, and a random ID made by the extension. Only sent when you choose to post a score.A Supabase database run by the developer. Stored until deleted.
Tag gameYour first name and last initial, your character's colour and hat, and your moves during the game.Passed through Supabase to the other players in your lobby. Not saved.
Chat RoomsYour first name and last initial, and the messages you send.Passed through Supabase to the other people in your room. Not saved by the extension or the developer.
Ask AIThe questions you type and the conversation so far. Your name and Daymap information are not included.Free public AI services: LLM7 (llm7.io) and Pollinations (pollinations.ai). Their own privacy policies apply.
Bug Report & SuggestionsWhat you type, plus the extension version, your browser and the Daymap page you were on.Opens an email in your own email account. Nothing is sent unless you press Send there.
Font (Windows and other non-Apple computers)A normal request for a font file.jsDelivr, a public file service.

Like any website, these services can see your internet address when your browser connects to them.

What the developer never does

  • Sell or share your information with anyone
  • Show ads or track what you do
  • Collect analytics
  • Use your information for anything except making these features work

Other people can see

In Tag, Chat Rooms and on the Snake leaderboard, other players see your first name and last initial. Anyone in a chat room can screenshot what you write, so don't share private details.

Students and parents

This extension is made for high school students. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's leaderboard entry removed, or have a question, please email the developer.

Removing your information

  • Information on your device is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear your browser's site data for Daymap.
  • To remove your Snake leaderboard entry, email the developer.
  • Tag and Chat Rooms messages are not saved, so there is nothing to remove.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
